The atelier

Painted flat, hung by hand.

There is no printing press in the studio. There is a twelve-metre table, a row of pigment jars, a stack of cartoons and Eleanor, with a brush.

  1. The room, at four in the afternoon

    We come to you and measure every drop, then sit in the room while the light goes. The colour is mixed for that light, not for the studio's.

  2. Cartoon and sample

    The pattern is drawn at full size for your wall heights. You get a 60 × 60 cm sample painted on your chosen ground within two weeks.

  3. Grinding the colour

    Pigment is ground into casein, gouache or linseed by hand and matched to the joinery, the floor and whatever you are not planning to change.

  4. Painting the lengths

    Each length is laid flat and painted freehand from the cartoon. Every fern, fruit and fan is one brushstroke, which is why no two drops repeat exactly.

  5. Curing and numbering

    Lengths cure for a week, then are rolled and numbered in hanging order so the pattern reads across corners, chimney breasts and doors.

  6. Hanging

    Our own paperhanger fits the paper, working around your decorators. Ceilings included, as long as someone holds the ladder.

Commissions

Bespoke, from a single wall to a whole house.

Most of what leaves the studio is made for one room. Interior designers and architects across Notting Hill, Holland Park and Kensington bring us the plan; we bring the paper.

A commission starts with a conversation and a site visit. We look at the proportions of the room, where the windows are, what the joinery is doing and what the floor wants to talk to. Then Eleanor draws a cartoon at full height for those exact walls, so a motif never lands awkwardly on a light switch or dies in a corner.

Existing patterns can be recoloured, rescaled or redrawn. New patterns can be drawn from your garden, a piece of fabric, a grandmother's plate. Ceilings are welcome; they are, if anything, the best part. Papers can be painted to run continuously across cupboard doors and panelling.

Trade clients receive a bound sample book, trade pricing and a named contact who will pick up the phone on site.
